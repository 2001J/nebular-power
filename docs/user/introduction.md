# Introduction

This system helps solar providers and customers manage small‑scale solar installations offered on financing plans. It monitors energy production and consumption, tracks payment compliance, and detects possible tampering so providers can protect assets and customers can keep systems running smoothly.

Purpose
- Give customers a clear view of their installation: energy charts, usage history, and upcoming payments.
- Help providers automate payment reminders and service status changes based on compliance.
- Detect and surface tampering or security issues early for quick resolution.

Key features
- Real‑time energy monitoring (recent, history, summaries)
- Payment dashboards, upcoming payments, and in‑app payment flow
- Compliance analytics and reminders (admin)
- Tamper detection alerts and security audit logs (admin)
- Service control actions: suspend/restore/start/stop (admin)

Benefits
- Better visibility into energy usage and system performance
- Automated compliance tracking reduces manual work
- Early detection of issues protects both customers and providers

Where it’s used
- Residential and small commercial solar systems sold via installment/lease models that require remote monitoring and compliance controls.
