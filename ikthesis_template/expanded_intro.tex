\section{Motivation}
\label{sec:motivation}
Solar energy represents one of the most promising renewable energy solutions in our collective effort to combat climate change and reduce dependency on fossil fuels. The technology has evolved dramatically over the past decades, with improved efficiency and reduced manufacturing costs. However, despite these advances, the high upfront cost of solar installation remains a significant barrier for residential and small commercial users, particularly in developing regions and for lower-income households.

To address this accessibility gap, many companies have introduced innovative financing models that allow users to pay for solar installations through installment plans. These pay-as-you-go or lease-to-own models have successfully expanded the reach of solar energy to previously underserved markets. However, these financing arrangements introduce new challenges for solar providers:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Payment Compliance Monitoring}: Tracking and managing customer payment schedules across numerous installations.
    \item \textbf{System Performance Monitoring}: Ensuring systems operate optimally to deliver the promised energy benefits.
    \item \textbf{Tampering Detection}: Identifying unauthorized modifications or manipulations of the system that might compromise its functionality or payment mechanisms.
    \item \textbf{Remote Management}: Enabling providers to monitor and control installations without requiring costly on-site visits.
\end{itemize}

The Solar Energy Monitoring and Control System addresses these challenges by providing a comprehensive, scalable solution for remotely monitoring solar energy systems, tracking consumption patterns, ensuring payment compliance, and detecting tampering attempts. By automating these processes, the solution reduces the operational overhead for solar providers while making solar energy more accessible to a broader range of users.

\section{Purpose of the System}
\label{sec:purpose}
The Solar Energy Monitoring and Control System platform is a comprehensive system designed to monitor, analyze, and manage solar energy installations. The system bridges the gap between solar providers and customers by providing real-time monitoring capabilities, financial management tools, and security features that protect both the provider's assets and the customer's investment.

The primary goals of the system are to:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Monitor solar energy production and consumption} in real-time across multiple installations, providing insights into system performance and usage patterns.
    \item \textbf{Generate detailed energy summaries} (daily, weekly, monthly) to track performance over time, identify anomalies, and optimize energy usage.
    \item \textbf{Manage financial aspects} of solar installations, including payment plans, transaction processing, and compliance monitoring.
    \item \textbf{Provide analytics and insights} to both providers and customers, enabling data-driven decisions about energy usage and system management.
    \item \textbf{Ensure security and compliance} through robust tampering detection, authentication mechanisms, and adherence to relevant regulations.
\end{enumerate}

By integrating these capabilities into a single platform, the Solar Energy Monitoring and Control System enables solar providers to scale their operations efficiently while maintaining high levels of service quality and security.

\section{Key Features}
\label{sec:keyfeatures}

\subsection{Energy Monitoring}
\label{subsec:energymonitoring}

The Energy Monitoring module forms the core of the Solar Energy Monitoring and Control System platform, providing comprehensive visibility into the performance and output of solar installations:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Real-time Monitoring}: Continuous tracking of energy production and consumption metrics, with readings collected at configurable intervals.
    \item \textbf{Historical Data Analysis}: Storage and visualization of historical energy data, enabling trend analysis and performance comparisons.
    \item \textbf{Automated Summaries}: Generation of daily, weekly, and monthly energy summaries, providing aggregated views of system performance.
    \item \textbf{Performance Metrics}: Calculation of key performance indicators such as efficiency ratios, yield rates, and consumption patterns.
    \item \textbf{Anomaly Detection}: Identification of unusual patterns or deviations from expected performance, triggering alerts for potential issues.
\end{itemize}



\subsection{Payment Compliance}
\label{subsec:paymentcompliance}

The Payment Compliance module manages the financial aspects of solar installations, particularly for systems using installment-based financing models:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Payment Plan Management}: Creation and management of customized payment plans tailored to different customer segments and installation types.
    \item \textbf{Transaction Processing}: Secure handling of payment transactions, providing flexible payment processing capabilities.
    \item \textbf{Automated Reminders}: Scheduled notifications for upcoming and overdue payments, reducing default rates through proactive communication.
    \item \textbf{Payment History}: Comprehensive records of payment transactions, providing both customers and administrators with transparent payment histories.
    \item \textbf{Compliance Reporting}: Generation of compliance reports for regulatory requirements and internal auditing purposes.
    \item \textbf{Grace Period Handling}: Flexible management of grace periods and late payment policies to accommodate customer circumstances.
\end{itemize}



\subsection{Security and Tampering Detection}
\label{subsec:security}

The Security and Tampering Detection module protects the integrity of solar installations and prevents unauthorized modifications:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Physical Tampering Detection}: Monitoring of sensors that detect unauthorized opening of equipment enclosures or modifications to hardware.
    \item \textbf{Electrical Anomaly Detection}: Identification of unusual voltage or current patterns that might indicate tampering with the electrical system.
    \item \textbf{Communication Security}: Encrypted communication channels between devices and the central platform, preventing man-in-the-middle attacks.
    \item \textbf{Geolocation Monitoring}: Tracking of device locations to detect unauthorized relocation of equipment.
    \item \textbf{Alert Management}: Generation and handling of security alerts, with configurable severity levels and response protocols.
    \item \textbf{Audit Logging}: Comprehensive logging of security events and system activities for forensic analysis and compliance purposes.
\end{itemize}



\subsection{User Management}
\label{subsec:usermanagement}

The User Management module handles authentication, authorization, and profile management for different user types:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Role-based Access Control}: Granular permission settings based on user roles (administrators, technicians, customers, etc.).
    \item \textbf{Profile Management}: Self-service tools for users to manage their profiles, contact information, and preferences.
    \item \textbf{Activity Tracking}: Monitoring and logging of user activities for security and audit purposes.
    \item \textbf{Communication Preferences}: Management of notification settings and communication channels for each user.
\end{itemize}

\section{System Architecture Overview}
\label{sec:architecture}

The Solar Energy Monitoring and Control System platform employs a modern, modular monolithic architecture that ensures maintainability, simplicity, and ease of deployment. The system is organized into several functional modules, each responsible for specific functionality:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Energy Monitoring Module}: Collects, processes, and analyzes energy production and consumption data from connected devices.
    \item \textbf{Payment Compliance Module}: Manages payment plans, processes transactions, and monitors compliance with payment schedules.
    \item \textbf{Security and Tampering Detection Module}: Monitors installations for unauthorized access or modifications, generating alerts for suspicious activities.
    \item \textbf{System Monitoring Module}: Tracks the health and status of connected devices, managing device heartbeats and diagnostics.
    \item \textbf{User Management Module}: Handles user authentication, authorization, and profile management.
    \item \textbf{Frontend Application}: Provides the user interface for different user roles, visualizing data and enabling system interaction.
\end{itemize}
